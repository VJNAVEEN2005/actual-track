Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 • Actual Track Application

Summary: Actual Track is 100% local-first and private. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any of your personal financial records or data.

1. Overview

Actual Track ("we", "us", or "the application") is a privacy-first, zero-based personal expense and budget management application available for Android and Windows desktop. Your financial privacy is the cornerstone of our software architecture.

2. Data Collection and Storage

Actual Track operates entirely locally on your device:

  • Local SQLite Database: All budgets, accounts, income entries, expense records, categories, and envelope allocations are stored exclusively in an encrypted or sandboxed SQLite database on your physical device.
  • No Cloud Accounts: You do not need to create an account, log in with an email, or submit personal identifiers to use the application.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: Actual Track contains no third-party trackers, diagnostic telemetry SDKs, or behavioral analytics packages.

3. Local Peer-to-Peer (Wi-Fi) Synchronization

Actual Track offers an optional peer-to-peer (P2P) sync feature between your smartphone and personal computer:

  • Sync operates strictly over your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot connection.
  • Data moves directly between device IP addresses on your private local area network (LAN).
  • Every sync session requires manual pairing through a dynamic 6-digit verification security PIN shown on the host screen.
  • No data is ever routed through external cloud servers or proxy relays during synchronization.

4. Third-Party Services

The core application does not transmit any information to third parties. If you download the application from the Google Play Store, Google may collect standard download and installation metrics in accordance with the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Security of Your Data

Because all data remains physically stored on your smartphone or PC, the security of your financial data depends on the security of your device (device passcode, fingerprint/face authentication, and operating system disk encryption).

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You have absolute control over your records at all times:

  • You may export or backup your data as a JSON file at any time from within the application Settings.
  • You may delete individual entries or clear all data permanently using the "Clear All Data" feature in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device completely wipes all locally stored SQLite database files.

7. Children's Privacy

Actual Track does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13. The application does not collect any personally identifiable information from any user.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices in future versions, we will post the revised policy on this webpage with an updated effective date.
