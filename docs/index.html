Features

Everything you need to master your money

Eight powerful modules, one beautifully simple app.

Zero-Based Envelope Budgeting

"Give every dollar a job" allocation with a real-time Free to Budget KPI so you always know what's left.

Responsive Desktop & Mobile

Desktop sidebar navigation, mobile bottom dock, and a 4-metric KPI banner that adapts to every screen.

Local-First SQLite Engine

Cross-platform SQLite with zero network latency. Your financial data never leaves your device.

Spending Analytics & Visuals

Smooth cash flow spline charts, interactive donut breakdowns, and category-level spending insights.

Multi-Account Liquidity

Checking, savings, investment, credit, and cash wallets with automatic reconciliation.

AI Financial Advisor

Gemini-powered chat with voice input & TTS. Supports 3.8 Flash, 2.5 Flash/Pro, and 2.0 Flash models.

Financial Calendar & Schedules

Recurring bills, payday forecasts, and monthly ledger dates so you never miss a payment.

Settings & Customization

API key config, model selection, voice settings, and full data import/export for total control.
